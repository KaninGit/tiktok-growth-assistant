TikTok Growth Assistant

A Windows desktop app that helps creators understand and plan content for their own TikTok account.

Analytics

Profile stats, video views, likes, comments, shares and engagement rate, with follower trends over time.

Insights

Best days and hours to post, hashtag performance and ideal video length, based on your own videos.

Scheduling

Prepare videos on your computer and post them to your own account at a chosen time, or send them to your TikTok inbox as drafts.

Local & private

Runs on your PC. Your data stays on your computer and is only exchanged with TikTok's official API.

How it works

  • Sign in with your TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit. You choose which permissions to grant.
  • The app reads your profile and video statistics through the TikTok Display API.
  • When you schedule a post, you choose the caption, who can view it and the interaction settings. The app then uploads it via the TikTok Content Posting API at the scheduled time.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the app's Settings, or remove access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

The app does not sell followers, automate following/unfollowing, or generate artificial engagement.
