Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTok Growth Assistant (the "App"), provided by Barrel Of Excellence, handles your information. The App is a desktop application that runs on your computer. We do not operate any server that receives your data.

1. Information the App accesses

When you connect your TikTok account and grant permission, the App accesses the following through TikTok's official APIs:

  • Profile information (user.info.basic, user.info.profile): open ID, display name, username, avatar, bio and profile link.
  • Account statistics (user.info.stats): follower, following, like and video counts.
  • Video list (video.list): your videos' IDs, titles/captions, post time, duration, cover image, share link and view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Content posting (video.publish, video.upload): used only to upload videos that you select and schedule, and to check their posting status.

The App also stores the video files' locations, captions and settings for posts you schedule.

2. How the information is used

  • To show your analytics, follower trends and posting insights inside the App.
  • To publish or send to your TikTok inbox the videos you schedule, with the settings you choose.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than providing the App's features to you.

3. Where information is stored

  • All data is stored locally on your computer in the App's data folder (on Windows: %APPDATA%\TikTok Growth Assistant).
  • TikTok access tokens and your app credentials are encrypted using the operating system's secure storage (Windows DPAPI).
  • Your data is sent only to TikTok (open.tiktokapis.com and TikTok upload endpoints) to perform the actions you request. It is not sent to Barrel Of Excellence or any other third party.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Barrel Of Excellence has no access to the data stored on your computer.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data remains on your computer until you delete it.
  • Clicking Disconnect in the App revokes the App's TikTok access token and removes it from your computer.
  • To delete all App data, uninstall the App and delete its data folder.
  • You can also remove the App's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

6. Your rights

Because your data is held on your own device, you can access, export (CSV) or delete it at any time. If you have questions or requests under applicable data protection laws, including Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), contact us below.

7. Children

The App is not intended for users under the minimum age required to use TikTok in their country.

8. Security

We use encrypted storage for credentials, HTTPS for all communication with TikTok, and a sandboxed app interface. No method of storage is completely secure. Please protect access to your computer.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will show the latest version.
